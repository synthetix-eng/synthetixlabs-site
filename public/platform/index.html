HOW IT WORKS

The Pipeline — Agents flow work between stages with confidence-scored evidence at every step.

Discover

Agents ingest the brief, the estate, or the incident — identifying scope, constraints, and regulatory posture, and flagging what's known versus what needs investigation. (Scout & Cartographer)

Analyze

Path-level comprehension. Cartographer builds the Atlas graph across code, IaC, topology, integrations, and data, identifying risk vectors with confidence scores. (Cartographer & Scout)

Architect

Target-state architecture generation synthesized from verified estate intelligence, infrastructure footprint, and business intent. (Architect, Estimator & Critic)

Build

Generation agents work in parallel waves to produce code, infrastructure, integrations. Conductor maintains delivery rhythm; Mentor surfaces guidance in-IDE. (Builder Swarm, Conductor & Mentor)

Verify

Examiner generates and runs tests. Coverage threshold gating. Failed units route back to the swarm. Performance against your SLAs measured before promotion. (Examiner &Critic)

Govern

Gatekeeper enforces policy, change classification, and audit. Provenance recorded for every decision. Human approval at promotion. Reversible by design. (Gatekeeper)

Conductor

Manages delivery state, parallelism, dependencies, and wave coordination across every active engagement. Conductor is the runtime that makes the pipeline repeatable, observable, and reversible — scheduling agent runs, routing outputs between stages, enforcing gates, and giving you a portfolio view across engagements. KNOWLEDGE

Atlas

A cross-domain, queryable model of your estate — code, infrastructure, topology, integrations, and data lineage as one connected world. Most AI tools see the file in the editor. Atlas sees the entire estate as one graph: every function call, every IaC dependency, every API contract, every data table, every service mesh route. Agents query Atlas before they act.

Book a Demo
//01

What Atlas Indexes

Atlas indexes application code at path level across every supported language; infrastructure as code, including Terraform, Ansible, Helm, and CloudFormation; network and service topology, including K8s and VMware; integration contracts such as REST, GraphQL, message buses, and batch feeds; and data lineage across schemas, lakes, and streams.

//02

What You Get

You get an estate intelligence dashboard with concentration mapping, drift detection between declared and observed reality, blast-radius queries for any proposed change, a provenance trace from any artifact back to its origin, and cross-engagement institutional memory that compounds.

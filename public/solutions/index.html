WHAT AGENTS DO

From Greenfield to Legacy: Every Stage of Delivery, Covered.

Greenfield Development

Synthetix doesn't assist engineers — it deploys an agent team that owns delivery end to end: requirements, architecture, build, test, and governance. Every output is gated by the Critic agent before it reaches a human. You direct outcomes, not keystrokes.

Code Modernization

Modernization fails at discovery, not delivery — months lost to SME interviews and stale docs before real work starts. Synthetix compresses that into days, mapping legacy estates across VB6, COBOL, and mainframe, then migrating at scale in parallel waves under full governance.

Application Support

Production support drains disproportionate senior engineering time on high-volume, repetitive work. Synthetix flips that model — agents triage, investigate, and propose evidence-backed fixes, escalating to engineering only when real judgment is needed.

Infrastructure Support

Configuration drift accumulates silently across cloud, hybrid, and on-prem environments: faster than manual review can keep up. Synthetix operates at the estate level instead, with continuous drift detection, policy-gated remediation, and zero ungated changes reaching production.
